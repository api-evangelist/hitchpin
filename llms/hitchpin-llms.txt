# HitchPin

> HitchPin is an online marketplace for the agriculture and rural economy, connecting farmers, ranchers, and rural businesses to buy and sell hay and forage, grain, cattle and livestock, farm and ranch equipment, trailers, land, and custom agricultural services. Sellers run branded storefronts, send instant invoices, and get paid; buyers browse and purchase across the US. HitchPin is backed by Foundry Group.

## APIs
- [Django API (instant invoicing)](https://apiv2.hitchpin.com): Marketplace and instant-invoicing REST API. OpenAPI 3.1.
- [Receipt Rendering Service](https://receipt-printing.prod.hitchpin.zone): Generates AOT receipts for secure retrieval. OpenAPI 3.1.
- [URL Shortcode Service](https://hitchp.in): Short-URL creation with expiration. OpenAPI 3.1.
